About

I am a Research Fellow at a government research organization in Islamabad, Pakistan, working at the intersection of Machine Learning, Physics-Informed Computing, and Aerodynamics. My work spans from developing Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) for solving complex PDEs to fine-tuning Large Language Models and building production-grade RAG systems.

With a strong foundation in PyTorch and TensorFlow, I thrive on problems that bridge traditional scientific computing and modern AI. My research contributions include published work on airfoil shape optimization using deep networks integrated with genetic algorithms, as well as interdisciplinary studies applying data analysis methodologies to social-science research questions.
